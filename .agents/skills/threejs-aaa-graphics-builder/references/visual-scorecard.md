# Visual Critique Prompts

This retained reference is optional critique vocabulary. It is not a numerical
completion gate. In Neva, `04` §19 owns visual acceptance and `03` §4 owns checks;
human approval cannot be inferred from an agent assessment.

## Inspect the requested result

Compare the affected asset or scene with its brief at the intended gameplay
camera. Select relevant questions:

- Do silhouette, proportions and spacing communicate the intended subject?
- Do form, palette, material response and lighting belong to the same world?
- Does detail remain purposeful and readable at gameplay distance?
- Can players identify the action, route, state and feedback during motion?
- Do relevant angles, lighting conditions, LODs or viewports expose a defect?
- Has the change introduced temporal instability or a measured resource problem?

Empty space, flat colors, simplified geometry and restrained effects can be
intentional. Do not require enemies, three obstacle variants, two rewards,
imported hero assets or dense set dressing in every genre or scoped task.
The archived `assets/scorecard-anchors/` images illustrate different scenes;
they are not Neva's visual authority or universal quality rankings.

## Evidence and review

Use focused before/after views when they clarify the change. Inspect motion for
temporal claims, and use renderer and production measurements for cost claims.
Pixel entropy, edge density, contrast and dominant-color share can help diagnose
blank or changed output; they cannot measure taste, authorship or premium quality.
Do not optimize the image toward these numbers.

Review your own result for the most plausible remaining defect. An independent
reviewer is useful when explicitly authorized, but not a compulsory phase. State
findings and their evidence directly; no adversarial essay or duplicated scores
are required. If the user explicitly asks for a score, agree a task-relevant
rubric, label it subjective and exclude irrelevant categories; a score still
does not close project acceptance gates.

## Handoff

Report the visible improvement, relevant inspection evidence and unresolved
defects or access gaps. Follow the repository completion format; no category
average or fixed report template is required.
